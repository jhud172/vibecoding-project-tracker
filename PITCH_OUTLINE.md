# Relay — five-minute pitch outline

- **0:00–0:40 · The problem**
  - AI coding leaves decisions scattered across chats and notes.
  - Returning later means reconstructing what worked and what to do next.
- **0:40–1:10 · The solution**
  - Relay turns team notes into Resume Capsules: a saved stopping point.
  - Explain the three steps: add notes → shape the handoff → review and save.
- **1:10–2:15 · Show a capsule**
  - Paste short, non-private example notes.
  - Extract recognised headings and show Goal, Decisions and Next step.
  - Record an unrun check honestly, then save.
- **2:15–3:15 · The main demonstration**
  - Show Take off here and the revision comparison warning.
  - Copy the reviewed handoff into a text editor or AI chat draft; no sending needed.
  - Explain that a new session preserves the earlier capsule and resets check results.
- **3:15–4:00 · Why choose Relay?**
  - A focused continuation workflow for students and developers using different AI tools.
  - Original notes, decisions, next action and reported evidence stay together.
  - Briefly show the four-stage task board and submission links; perform the task Context-copy demo if required.
- **4:00–4:35 · Design, access and reliability**
  - Show one accessibility preference and the design justification guide.
  - Mention original Blender artwork, saved drafts, backups and recovery.
  - Explain that animation is optional and task information remains ordinary text.
- **4:35–5:00 · Evidence, limits and close**
  - Built solo on the supplied starter; tested workflows and live deployment.
  - Results are user-reported; Relay does not inspect code or execute tests.
  - Cloud remains a preview; human accessibility evaluation and your timed rehearsal are separate.
  - Close: “Relay keeps the decisions and next action ready for your next session.”
